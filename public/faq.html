Everything to know about the BLX shopping demo.

Can I buy shoes on this website?

BLX STORE is a shopping demonstration. You can explore the collection, select options and try the bag and checkout flow, but no real order or payment is processed. You must sign in before reviewing the demo checkout.

What currency are the prices in?

All prices are shown in US dollars (USD). Prices are illustrative for this concept and are not live retail offers.

How do I select a colour?

Open a product, choose one of its available colour options and then select your EU size. The selected colour appears in your bag and order summary. Where a product has one pictured colourway, only that option is offered.

What sizing system do you use?

The demo uses EU sizes 38–46. Sizes are sample choices and do not represent verified inventory. For a real purchase, always consult the manufacturer’s sizing guide.

Can I change my bag?

Yes. Use the plus and minus controls to change quantities, or Remove to delete a pair. Different sizes and colours are kept as separate items.

Do you offer delivery or returns?

There are no deliveries or returns because this demo does not sell products. Shipping regions, rates and a return policy would be added before a real store launch.

Do I need an account?

You can browse, choose colours and sizes, and add shoes to your bag without signing in. Checkout requires secure sign-in with ChatGPT. The pop-up can be closed at any time. BLX does not collect passwords or card details. Your bag is kept for your current browser session.

Is BLX affiliated with the brands shown?

No. BLX STORE is an independent concept. Brand names identify the sneakers pictured; they do not imply a partnership or endorsement.
